🛡️

Safety comes first

Before enabling AI, we'll need a protected server-side connection, sensible usage limits, and safeguards against harmful or shaming responses.

🔐

Privacy by design

Never put an AI API key in public website code. We'll also explain what data is sent to the AI provider before any chat feature goes live.

Community guidelines

For a future moderated stories page.

  • ✓ Share what helped you, without claiming it works for everyone.
  • ✓ Be kind. No shame, insults, or pressure.
  • ✓ Keep identifying details and explicit content out of posts.
  • ✓ Respect privacy; never pressure anyone to share.
  • ✓ Ask a trusted adult for help if a post raises a safety concern.

A public community would need moderation, reporting, privacy controls, and age-appropriate safeguards before launch.
